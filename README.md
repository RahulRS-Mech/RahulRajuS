# 💫 About Me:
I’m a mechanical engineer currently working on large-scale equipment design, supporting Caterpillar.<br><br>Most of my work is around turning engineering ideas into something that can actually be built - developing CAD models, defining tolerances, and creating drawings that are clear and usable on the shop floor. I’ve come to really enjoy that space where design meets manufacturing - where things either work in the real world, or don’t.<br><br>Before this, I worked on experimental fluid systems and a motion platform project, which pushed me to think more about how systems behave in real conditions, not just in theory.<br><br>I use this space to share the work I’m doing and how I approach engineering problems. Always open to connecting with people working on interesting ideas and meaningful problems.
